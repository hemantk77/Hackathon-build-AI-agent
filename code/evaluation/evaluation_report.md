# Evaluation Report — Ghost Data Still Exists

All numbers below were produced by the commands in [Reproduction](#reproduction) on the final code. **No hidden answers were available or used.** Public-sample accuracy, custom-suite accuracy and internal consistency of the full output are separate measurements and are reported separately.

## 1. Metrics

The scorer is `code/evaluation/main.py`. It ignores JSON key order but respects array order where the metric says so.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Case-level status accuracy | Share of cases whose `deletion_status` matches. |
| Exact-case accuracy | Status and the full ordered step list match exactly (all 7 fields of every step). |
| Step precision / recall / F1 | Multiset match of steps on `(phase, system_id, resource_id, operation, state, evidence_id)`, ignoring `step_no`. |
| Field-level accuracy | Position-by-position agreement of all 7 fields over `max(len(pred), len(gold))` positions, so missing or extra steps count as wrong fields. |
| Sequence accuracy | Share of cases whose ordered list of `(phase, operation, system_id, resource_id)` matches exactly. |
| Sequence LCS | Mean longest-common-subsequence ratio of the same ordered keys (partial credit for order). |

## 4. Unit tests and the independent validator

- `python -m unittest discover -s code/tests`: **15 tests, all passing**. They cover:
  - multilingual and negated authorization;
  - all log line formats;
  - historical and case-insensitive identity, plus decoy rejection;
  - post-audit recreation;
  - non-citable late files;
  - replica drift and absence by omission;
  - policy decisions, including expired retention and tombstones;
  - ordering and numbering;
  - the validator rejecting a bad operation/state pair and a mis-ordered array;
  - the reference answers passing the validator.
- `code/validate_output.py` re-reads the raw CSVs and checks:
  - header, row count, id set, duplicates and natural sort;
  - status values and JSON parsing;
  - the exact 7 fields per step, consecutive `step_no`, and operation→(phase, state) pairs;
  - phase/operation/system/resource ordering and duplicate steps;
  - `NOT_APPLICABLE ⇒ []`, and `COMPLETE` containing only cited verification steps;
  - `INCOMPLETE` findings having evidence and future steps having an empty `evidence_id`;
  - every finding having a remediation and every remediation having a verification;
  - every evidence id existing (file, activity or audit id) and dated at or before the case's `audit_at`.

## 7. Known limitations and review items

- Unrecoverable references: 3 of 111 sample reference resources have no trace in participant data.
- Ties: 105 primary-account ties and 36 perfect-matching analytics ties are resolved by deterministic guess, with an expected accuracy of about 50% on those steps. They are listed in `--diagnostics` as `tie_guess`.
- About 47 anonymize events remain unassigned because the candidate requests are not a perfect matching; those verification steps are omitted rather than guessed.
- The hand-crafted scenario cases (screenshots and PDFs) have no matching public samples. Interpretations used:
  - catalog-linked record only;
  - a backup with no visible restore block counts as a restore risk;
  - a connector shown streaming counts as an active flow;
  - case_63 follows the catalog link even though the screenshot shows a different name.
- Category inference for rare records (hash-keyed analytics contact records → secondary identifier → delete; legacy support contact copies → delete; expired security retention → delete) is grounded in one sample or in the rules text only.
